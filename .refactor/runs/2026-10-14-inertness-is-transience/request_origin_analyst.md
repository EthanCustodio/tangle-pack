## The question

**What makes a bridge class inert?** Today inertness is a fixed point over REGISTERED image evidence (`_resolve_inertness`, `has_image_evidence` in `BridgeClass.py`): a class is inert iff it has at least one resolvable image and every resolvable image is a loop or in an inert class; a class with no registered image is active by the no-evidence convention. It is monotone in evidence, and it decides which hole bridges the minimal trellis maps (only the active ones). Author decision Q3 (I-a, 2026-10-09): inertness is TRANSIENCE in the word graph, computed AFTER the words, with every hole bridge mapped in the minimal trellis: a class is inert iff no infinite forward path leaves it in the class transition graph. The plan's probe (2026-10-09, pre-B1): class-level words unchanged on 14 builds; image bridges +0..2; the iterated partition `P1` finer on 6 builds.
