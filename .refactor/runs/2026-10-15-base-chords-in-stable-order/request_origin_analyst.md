## The question

**Which stretches of a lobe image's OWN stable branch are empty?** The iterated partition `P1 = P ∨ f(∂P)` (B7, `PartitionFamily.py` module docstring) declares the base and the chords of every stretched hole's image lobe `f(H)` empty. The interior crossings of the image chain pair into chords per stable branch: on any FOREIGN branch in STABLE order (C19, 2026-10-09: `f(H)` holds no anchor, so walking the branch from its anchor enters and leaves `f(H)` in turn — Jordan alternation; measured by point-in-polygon in `tests/regression/test_foreign_chords.py`), but on the BASE branch in CHAIN order, the order the curve visits them (the author's rule of 2026-09-23; `IteratedHomotopyPartition._empty_stretches`, `PartitionFamily.py:985-996`, the `if branch_key != base_branch: on_branch.sort(...)` test at `:993`).

B7 found the one place the two orders differ on the corpus and the chain order is wrong there. On `k32_series` at 6 and 7 blasts the hole `(6, 7)`'s lobe image `8→9→10→11→12→13` spirals back across its own base branch (stable order `13, 8, 11, 10, 9, 12`). Point-in-polygon of every stable gap's midpoint against the lobe image polygon (`f(b) ∪ σ'`) gives `(8,11)` out, `(11,10)` IN, `(10,9)` out, `(9,12)` IN: the covered stretches are the STABLE-order pairs `(11,10)` (already hole `(10,11)`'s stretch) and `(9,12)`. Chain order declares `(10,9)` and `(11,12)` empty instead, pinches crossing 10 into a singleton and a sibling member's image legitimately crosses `(10,9)` at `93, 94`. Consequence: in the class `{R_(0.0;8), R_(0.0;10)}` (positional `A:p1@0.0/R#7 <-> A:p1@0.0/R#9`, letter `k`) the hole-bridge member `(6, 7)` reads other `P1` sub-elements than its siblings `(54,55)`, `(64,65)`, `(96,97)` — Theorem T fails, the class-level word is identical, and the walk sides with the siblings. It is registered as the strict xfail `KNOWN_ISSUES[("series_members_share_the_P1_itinerary", "k32_series")]` (`tests/cases.py:1354-1361`, wp `B7b`).

The analyst's scratch patch (drop the `branch_key != base_branch` test, so the base branch's chords pair in stable order too) reran all 62 builds and steps: T holds everywhere (0 disagreements on k32 steps 6-7; the k28 "stable end" cases stay incomplete evidence, skipped), `M`, `M_1`, node names, class counts, active counts and `is_reliable` are identical on all 62, the in-splitting has 0 violations; what changes is the number of SPLIT INERT classes (−1 on 15 builds and steps: `heteroclinic`, `p3_16`, `p3_6_blasts`, nested with 6 inner blasts, `p3_series` 6-9, `k32_series` 2-5 and 8-10 — all outside both matrices) and iterated element NAMES wherever the two orders differ (only possible when a base branch carries 4 or more interior crossings; the k=2.8 fixture's single chord `(9,10)` is unaffected).
